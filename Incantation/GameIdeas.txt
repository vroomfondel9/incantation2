Guiding Principal Above All Else
================================
The purpose of this game is to deliver on the fantasy of what it's like to really be a wizard and have powers that can alter the laws of reality.

Ideas
=====
-The player stumbles upon fragments of instructions in the language of magic. 
	-Some parts are fixed, some variable. The player must figure out what they do themselves through experimentation.
	-They can record notes in their spellbook of what they think the spell fragment does. They can then build spells based on this fragment.

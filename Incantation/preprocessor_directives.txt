Volume-Level Physics Debug Visualizations:
=========================================
DEBUG_DRAW_BROADPHASE - Draws broadphase pairs (editor only)
DEBUG_DRAW_NARROWPHASE - Draws narrowphase bounding shapes (editor only)

Voxel-Level Physics Debug Visualizations:
=========================================
DEBUG_DRAW_EDGES - Draws motions projections and contact points for edges.
DEBUG_DRAW_CORNERS - Draws motions projections and contact points for corners.
DEBUG_DRAW_MOTION_PROJECTIONS - Draws voxel check projections - corner only though (editor only). Depends on DEBUG_DRAW_EDGES & DEBUG_DRAW_CORNERS.
DEBUG_DRAW_MOTION_PROJECTION_CHECKED_CELLS - Draws voxel DDA searched cells prior to finding a contact point - corner only though (editor only). Depends on DEBUG_DRAW_EDGES & DEBUG_DRAW_CORNERS.
DEBUG_DRAW_CONTACT_POINTS - Draws contact collision points (editor only). Depends on DEBUG_DRAW_EDGES & DEBUG_DRAW_CORNERS.
DEBUG_DRAW_CONTACT_NORMALS - Draws contact collision point normals (editor only). Depends on DEBUG_DRAW_CONTACT_POINTS, DEBUG_DRAW_EDGES & DEBUG_DRAW_CORNERS.

System or Subsystem-Wide Statistics:
=========================================
STATS_BROADPHASE - Gathers broadphase stats as a singleton. Type: BroadphaseStats
STATS_NARROWPHASE - Grathers narrowphase stats as a singleton. Type: NarrowphaseStats
STATS_ALLOCATOR_ORIGINAL - Gathers original volume allocator stats as a singleton. Type: OriginalVoxelHeapStats
STATS_ALLOCATOR_MODIFIED - Gathers modified volume allocator stats as a singleton.
